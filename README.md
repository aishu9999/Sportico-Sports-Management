\# Sportico – Sports Management System



Sportico is a web-based Sports Management System developed to provide a centralized platform for managing sports clubs, turfs, trainers, camps, sports facilities, shops, bookings, appointments, and related services.



\## 🚀 Project Overview



Sportico brings different sports and fitness-related services together in one platform.



The system provides separate functionalities for administrators, clubs, shops, trainers, and users.



Users can explore sports-related services, view club and turf information, book turfs and camps, purchase sports items, and interact with trainers.



Administrators can manage the overall system and maintain information related to clubs, shops, sports news, and other services.
